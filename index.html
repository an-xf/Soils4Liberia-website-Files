October 26–30, 2026 Monrovia, Liberia

From Soil Data to Sustainable Agriculture: IITA Leads Partners in Advancing Liberia's National Soil Information System

By Daniel Momo Sirleaf Sr.

Liberia's efforts to strengthen agricultural planning, improve soil management, and build a sustainable food system are receiving renewed attention as the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture (IITA), with support from the European Union (EU), leads national and international partners in advancing the Soils4Liberia Project.

The Annual Planning, Coordination and Stakeholder Engagement Workshop brought together government institutions, research organizations, soil information specialists and development partners to review implementation progress, strengthen collaboration and advance the development of Liberia's national soil information infrastructure.

Soils4Liberia Project Launch

IITA launches EU-funded Soils4Liberia project to strengthen soil health and food systems in Liberia

Soil is the basis of human existence. After decades of neglect, deforestation, and a lack of reliable data, a bold initiative launched in Monrovia is restoring hope and redefining how Liberia manages its land resources.

IITA–CGIAR, in partnership with the European Union (EU) and the Government of Liberia, officially launched the Liberia Land and Soil Resources Knowledge Project (Soils4Liberia) on 22 October 2025 to transform soil management through science, technology, and collaboration.
